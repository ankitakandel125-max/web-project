About us

Everest Valley School was founded to give every child a strong start in learning and in life. We combine solid academics with good values, discipline and kindness.

Our teachers know each student by name and work closely with parents to support progress at every grade.

Our mission

To help students become confident learners, responsible citizens and caring people.

Why choose us?

  • Experienced and caring teachers
  • Small class sizes
  • Safe and friendly campus
  • Regular parent-teacher meetings
